01 · Brand Essence

The meaning behind the name

The name

“Kalma”, from calm: the stillness you find when time slows down in the middle of Raja Ampat's sea.

Kalma is a homestay in Raja Ampat, Southwest Papua: a simple wooden house by crystal-clear water, run with local hospitality. We don't sell luxury. We offer a pause: waking up to the sound of waves, snorkeling right off your room, sharing grilled fish with your host family.

The wave that splits the logo into two colors is the waterline: above is the bright lagoon, below is the calm depth of the sea. The letters curve like palm fronds and fish fins.

02 · Logo

Logo anatomy

A hand-lettered wordmark split by a two-tone wave. The logo is our most valuable asset: never redraw it, always use the official files.

① Wordmark ② Wave line ③ Descriptor

① Wordmark “Kalma” Organic letters that curve like leaves and fins. The shapes are unique and must never be replaced by another font.

② Wave line Splits the letters into Lagoon (top) and Deep Sea (bottom). It is Kalma's signature and the source of every wave graphic.

③ Descriptor “RAJA AMPAT” Widely tracked capitals that anchor the location. May be removed only in the wordmark version.

Note: the logo is currently a high-resolution PNG. For large print (banners, signage) use a vector version (SVG/AI/PDF).

07 · Tone of Voice

Tone of voice

Kalma speaks like a friendly host picking guests up at the pier: relaxed, warm, never over the top.

Calm, not lazy

Short sentences, slow rhythm, no strings of exclamation marks.

Warm, not cheesy

Speak to “you”; skip empty superlatives.

Honest & clear

Say it as it is: power, signal, travel time.

Local & caring

Add local words and a reminder to protect the sea.
